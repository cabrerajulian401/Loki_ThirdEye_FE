Scout — Support

Scout is an Outlook add-in by ThirdEye that keeps purchase orders on track from inside your inbox: live order tracking, automated vendor check-ups, thread audits that flag discrepancies, and Otto — an AI assistant that records confirmed order data to your ERP.

Common questions

Scout asks for permissions — why? Scout reads order-related email threads and sends scheduled check-up messages on your behalf. Your organization's admin can approve these permissions once for everyone; otherwise you'll see a one-time Microsoft consent prompt on first use.

The pane shows no orders. Scout needs your mailbox connected and an initial sync to complete. If the state persists, contact support with a screenshot.

How do I remove Scout? Outlook → Apps → manage add-ins, or ask your Microsoft 365 admin if it was deployed organization-wide.
