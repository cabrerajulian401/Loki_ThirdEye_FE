Scout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Scout is an Outlook add-in by ThirdEye ("we", "us") that helps purchasing teams track orders, run vendor check-ups, and audit email threads for discrepancies. This policy describes what data Scout processes and how it is handled.

Data we access

With your consent (or your organization's admin consent), Scout accesses your Microsoft 365 mailbox through Microsoft Graph, limited to the permissions shown at sign-in: reading mail relevant to order tracking and sending scheduled check-up messages on your behalf. Authentication uses your existing Microsoft sign-in; Scout never sees or stores your password.

Data we store

Scout's service stores the working data the product needs: order records and their status, check-up schedules, audit findings, and references to the email threads they came from. This data is stored in our cloud environment hosted on Microsoft Azure.

AI processing

To extract order details and detect discrepancies, relevant email content is processed by a large-language-model provider (Anthropic) under commercial API terms. This content is used only to produce the extraction or finding returned to you and is not used to train models.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except the service providers named above (Microsoft Azure hosting, Anthropic AI processing) as needed to operate Scout.

Retention and deletion

Order, check-up, and audit records are retained while your account is active. To request export or deletion of your data, email julian.cabrera@yendo.com; we honor requests within 30 days. Revoking Scout's permissions in your Microsoft account settings immediately ends mailbox access.
