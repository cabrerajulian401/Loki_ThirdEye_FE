Black & white wireframes for first-run setup. Chosen direction: a setup checklist that signs the user in, orients them, and connects their records before landing on Suppliers. Every screen is the real Outlook task-pane width (340px).
MICROSOFT SSO SETUP CHECKLIST B&W

Set-and-forget, but transparent. Each step's message and relay timing are editable; the activity timeline shows exactly who was emailed and when, and a reply auto-pauses the check so vendors never get nagged after responding.
Always on. Scout audits open threads in the background and only interrupts when it finds something — a notification plus a red dot on the Audit tab. Nothing to remember to run.
Grouped by conversation. Each finding keeps its type icon ($ price, ⏱ time, ▣ item) and severity, and taps through to the exact quotes that conflict.
Confirm-first, always. Otto never writes silently — the user reviews, edits inline, or corrects via chat before anything reaches the ERP, then verifies after. Directly answers the PRD risk of blind trust.
Why a checklist: SMB users set up at their own pace and feel in control. The unfinished tasks ride along into the Home card as a progress nudge, so onboarding never feels like a dead-end wizard.
